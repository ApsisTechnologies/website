### The tech behind our machine learning solutions

We leverage the latest in machine learning frameworks and technologies to build solutions that are fast, scalable, and incredibly effective.

- **<a href="https://www.python.org/" target="_blank">Python</a> & <a href="https://www.langchain.com/" target="_blank">Langchain</a>**: We use Python for its flexibility and efficiency, while Langchain helps us imbue language models with real-world skills so that they can act on your behalf.

- **<a href="https://pytorch.org/" target="_blank">PyTorch</a>**: For developing and training deep learning models that are both flexible and scalable, ensuring our solutions adapt to the demands of your growing business.

- **<a href="https://scikit-learn.org/stable/index.html" target="_blank">Scikit-Learn</a>**: Our go-to for traditional ML algorithms like classification and regression, it allows us to quickly develop models that are robust and reliable.

- **<a href="https://kubernetes.io/" target="_blank">Kubernetes</a> + <a href="https://www.docker.com/" target="_blank">Docker</a>**: We scale and deploy machine learning models in the cloud effortlessly with containerization technologies, ensuring that your ML solution is always running at peak efficiency and stay fully available at global scale.
